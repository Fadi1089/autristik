The current MP3 music files were supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-13 and moved into
this directory without changing their contents. They are separate from the CC0 effects.

Music streams locally through an HTML audio element, with native looping and volume control.
Pausing or restarting a game keeps the same player and playhead. Playback is attempted on app
mount; a browser autoplay rejection is retried on the next click or key press.

Menu navigation uses `menu-nav.ogg`, originally `click_002.ogg` from
[Kenney — Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), under CC0.
Playback lowers its pitch to 75% speed, filters high frequencies above 1.2 kHz, and adds a
6 ms attack / 12 ms release envelope for a softer cue. The source OGG is unchanged.

The retained former track `bird-in-hand-night.ogg` is `bird_in_hand_night_0.ogg` by
[Fupi — Bird in Hand Night](https://opengameart.org/content/bird-in-hand-night), under CC0 1.0.
Fupi describes this as a soft, calming jazz/classical-rock variation with the drums removed,
based on Jason Shaw's CC0 _Bird in Hand_. The source page explicitly lists CC0 for this recording.
The original lasts 192.386 seconds (3:12).

The retained former track `sunset-walk.ogg` is `SunsetWalk.ogg` by
[Kilua Boy — Sunset Walk](https://opengameart.org/node/87082), also under CC0.

The water-drop option uses a short bubbling/plop clip from rubberduck's pack.
Kenney's original license notices are included in this directory.
rubberduck's source page explicitly lists CC0 as the license.

[CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Special-tile cues (void suction, gold, bedrock, bronze, silver and diamond) are original
procedural effects generated by `src/audio/tileSounds.ts`, using shaped noise and damped
oscillators. They do not use additional recordings or external assets.

The Time-attack watch tick/tock loop is an original procedural effect generated by
`src/audio/clockTick.ts`. It uses alternating damped tones and filtered noise, with no
external recording or download.

Game-over, record and low-supply cues are also original procedural effects generated by
`src/audio/gameCueSounds.ts`: a descending motif, an ascending arpeggio and a 160 ms double
beep. No additional recordings or external assets are used.

The supply alert uses two softened bass tones (180 Hz and 140 Hz), with no bright harmonics
and a lower amplitude than the original electronic cue. It remains 160 ms long.
